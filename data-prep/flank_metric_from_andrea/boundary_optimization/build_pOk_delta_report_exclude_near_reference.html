pOk: Original vs. Extended Definitions

Evaluating 7,286 extended locus definitions, genotyping them (and the original definitions) in 106 genome samples from 1kGP. Mean ΔpOk = +0.1468, median = +0.0560. By per-locus mean ΔpOk (averaged across its samples/alleles) -- Better (>+0.05): 4,558 loci (62.6%). Worse (<−0.05): 473 loci (6.5%). Flat: 2,255 loci (30.9%).
