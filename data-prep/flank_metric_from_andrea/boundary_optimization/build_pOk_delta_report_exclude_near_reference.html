pOk: Original vs. Extended Definitions

Evaluating 6,978 extended locus definitions, genotyping them (and the original definitions) in 106 genome samples from 1kGP. Mean ΔpOk = +0.1302, median = +0.0390. By per-locus mean ΔpOk (averaged across its samples/alleles) -- Better (>+0.05): 3,973 loci (56.9%). Worse (<−0.05): 526 loci (7.5%). Flat: 2,479 loci (35.5%).

106 1kGP samples (up to 3 per Population/Gender group) · near-reference alleles excluded -- alleles within ±2 repeats of the reference allele size (recomputed per locus definition) are dropped; both short and long alleles of a genotype are otherwise kept

Allele pairing: original/extended alleles are paired by AlleleNumber when both definitions report the same genotype cardinality. When cardinality differs between definitions (e.g. homozygous under one, heterozygous under the other -- 11.8% of paired locus/sample combinations in this dataset), the single allele is instead paired with the LARGER of the other definition's alleles (extending the reference window generally only grows measured allele size, so the larger allele is the more plausible continuation of the same physical copy); the smaller allele is left unpaired.
